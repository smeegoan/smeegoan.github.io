Privacy Policy for TrumpAgain

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

Introduction

TrumpAgain ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your information.

Information We Collect

TrumpAgain stores all your game progress (choices made, stats, unlocked achievements) locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share your personal information with external servers. There is no account, sign-up, or login of any kind.

The only third-party service in the app that collects data automatically is advertising, which collects the following:

  • Advertising ID: Used by AdMob for ad personalization and measurement

Data Storage

  • All game progress is stored locally on your device in a local database
  • No data is sent to external servers by our app
  • No user accounts or authentication required

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

Our app displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information for ad personalization and measurement purposes. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • App usage data related to ad interactions

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent prompt (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting personalized ads, and honors the choice you make there.

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

You can opt out of personalized ads by adjusting your device's ad settings.

Google Play In-App Review

After some milestones the app may show Google Play's rating prompt. It is run by Google Play: any rating or review you leave goes to Google Play under your Google account, and the app never sees it.

Feedback Emails

The "Send feedback" button on the main menu opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email. The draft includes a short diagnostics block you can read or delete before sending:

  • App version and build number
  • Operating system and version
  • App language
  • Your current campaign, day and story event, if a game is in progress

If you send it, we receive your email address and what you wrote. We use it only to reply and to fix the problem you report, we do not share it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to the address below.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number), except in a feedback email you choose to send
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Photos, videos, or files
  • Calendar or contacts
  • Payment information

Your game progress is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to any servers.

Children's Privacy

TrumpAgain is a satirical commentary on real-world politics, intended for a general adult audience and not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security and Encryption

Any data transmitted between your device and the AdMob service is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS protocols.

Data Deletion

Local Data (Game Progress): You can delete all your local app data at any time by:

  • Using the "Reset game" option in the Settings screen, or
  • Uninstalling the app (removes all local data from your device)

Data Collected by Google (AdMob): We do not store any of your data on our servers and cannot delete data collected by Google. To manage this data:

  • Stop future collection: Uninstall the app
  • Opt out of personalized ads: Device Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Request deletion: Contact Google directly at Google Privacy Support

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (stored locally on your device)
  • Delete your data (via app settings or uninstalling)
  • Opt out of personalized ads (via device settings)

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates when this policy was last revised.
