Privacy Policy for 30 Days Glow Up

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

Introduction

30 Days Glow Up ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your information.

Information We Collect

30 Days Glow Up stores all your personal data (goals, progress, preferences) locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share your personal information with external servers.

Third-party services integrated in the app collect the following data automatically:

  • Advertising ID: Used by AdMob for ad personalization and measurement

Data Storage

  • All goal completion data, including any reflection notes you write, is stored locally on your device using Hive (local database)
  • Your start date and progress are stored locally using SharedPreferences
  • No data is sent to external servers by our app
  • No user accounts or authentication required
  • Daily reminders are scheduled on your device; no server is involved
  • A share card image is created on your device and leaves it only when you share it yourself through Android's share menu

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

Our app displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain information for ad personalization and measurement purposes. This may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • App usage data related to ad interactions

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options, or opt out of personalized ads in your device's ad settings.

Google Play In-App Review

After some milestones the app may show Google Play's rating prompt. It is run by Google Play: any rating or review you leave goes to Google Play under your Google account, and the app never sees it.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Photos, videos, or files
  • Calendar or contacts
  • Payment information

Your goals, notes, and progress are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to any servers.

Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Data Security and Encryption

All data transmitted between your device and third-party services (AdMob) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS protocols.

Data Deletion

Local Data (Goals & Progress): You can delete all your local app data at any time by:

  • Using the "Reset Progress" option in the Settings screen, or
  • Uninstalling the app (removes all local data from your device)

Data Collected by Google (AdMob): We do not store any of your data on our servers and cannot delete data collected by Google services. To manage this data:

  • Stop future collection: Uninstall the app
  • Opt out of personalized ads: Device Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Request deletion: Contact Google directly at Google Privacy Support

Note: Google automatically deletes analytics data according to their retention policies (typically 60-180 days after your last activity).

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (stored locally on your device)
  • Delete your data (via app settings or uninstalling)
  • Opt out of personalized ads (in Settings → Privacy options, or your device settings)

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and within the app. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates when this policy was last revised.
