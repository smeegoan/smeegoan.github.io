Clean Scans

Edge detection, cropping, filters and shadow removal. Import photos or PDFs, or share them in from any app, and touch up pages with rotate, crop and black and white.

Searchable PDFs

Text is read on your phone and laid invisibly over each page, so you can search and copy it in any PDF reader.

Receipts to Spreadsheets

Merchant, date, total and tax filled in for you, duplicates flagged and totals shown per month. Export any period as Excel or CSV, with the receipt PDFs zipped alongside.

🏷️

Invoice QR Codes

Reads SEPA payment codes (GiroCode), Swiss QR-bills and Portuguese invoice codes for exact amounts.

Your Own Server

Send scans to Nextcloud, ownCloud, any WebDAV server or Paperless-ngx, book receipts in Firefly III, or email them to an expense inbox. Automatic if you like, on Wi-Fi only, and retried when you're back online.
