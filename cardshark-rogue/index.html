Privacy Policy for CardShark Rogue

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

Introduction

CardShark Rogue ("we", "our", or "the app") is a collection of classic card games played against computer opponents, against people nearby, or online. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, where it goes, and how you can remove it.

Information Stored on Your Device

Game progress (ladder rungs, achievements, statistics, unlocked decks and backgrounds), your settings and your saved matches are stored locally on your device. Nothing in this category is sent to our servers. There is no account, sign-up or login to play offline.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android version is free and shows advertisements through Google AdMob: a banner on the Ladder screen and in the nearby and online lobbies while you wait for other players, and an optional rewarded video you can choose to watch in Options to unlock a card deck or table background. AdMob may collect the following automatically for ad delivery, personalization and measurement:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse location derived from it
  • Usage data related to ad interactions

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the app shows a consent prompt (Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting personalized ads and honours your choice. You can reopen it at any time from the "Privacy options" row in Options. You can also opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

Google's own policy: Google Privacy Policy.

Online Play (Epic Online Services)

Online tables and rankings are optional. When you open the online lobby the app signs you in as a guest through Epic Online Services (EOS) using a device-bound identifier; no email, name or Epic account is required. EOS provides the lobby, matchmaking and the peer-to-peer relay that carries card plays between players. EOS receives your IP address and the guest identifier to do this. Epic's policy: Epic Games Privacy Policy.

Rankings Service

When a public ranked table finishes, each player's app sends the match outcome to our rankings service (hosted on Cloudflare). We store, keyed by the pseudonymous EOS guest identifier:

  • The display name and avatar you chose for the leaderboard (never verified; pick anything)
  • Your rating, games played, wins, losses and draws per game
  • Match records (participants, seats, placements, rules and seed hashes), kept for 90 days for dispute resolution and then deleted automatically

The leaderboard shows display names, avatars and ratings to other players. No chat is stored. Private and nearby tables never contact the rankings service.

Nearby Play (Wi-Fi and Bluetooth)

Nearby tables connect devices directly over the local Wi-Fi network or Bluetooth Low Energy. The app asks for Bluetooth permissions, and on some Android versions the location permission that Android requires for Bluetooth scanning. The app does not read, store or transmit your location. Nearby play involves no server.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Name, email address or phone number
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, files or calendar
  • Payment information (the app has no purchases)

Children's Privacy

CardShark Rogue includes simulated casino games (Blackjack, poker variants) played with virtual chips and no real money. It is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

All traffic between the app and AdMob, Epic Online Services and the rankings service is encrypted in transit with HTTPS or the EOS transport encryption.

Data Deletion

  • Local data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Rankings data: Options > Online > "Erase online data" deletes your ratings and replaces your display name with "Deleted player" on our service immediately. Match records expire on their own within 90 days.
  • AdMob data: we hold none of it. Opt out of personalization in Device Settings > Google > Ads, or contact Google Privacy Support.
  • EOS guest identity: uninstalling the app abandons the device-bound guest identifier; Epic's retention is described in their policy.

Your Rights

You may access, correct (display name and avatar) and delete the online data described above from inside the app, and you may withdraw ad consent at any time. If you need help with any of this, contact us at the address below.
