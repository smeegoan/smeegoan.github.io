Privacy Policy for Night of Souls

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

Introduction

Night of Souls ("we", "our", or "the app") is a branching gamebook set in a Portuguese mountain village on the night of the dead. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information We Collect

Night of Souls does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not send anything to a server.

Data Storage

  • Your saved game (current passage, Fear, codewords and journal), unlocked achievements and settings (language, text speed, sound, music) are stored locally on your device
  • The illustration tilt effect reads the device's motion sensors while the app is open; those readings are used on the device and never stored or sent anywhere
  • No user accounts or sign-in

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, videos, or files
  • App usage or diagnostics
  • Payment information

Children's Privacy

Night of Souls is a horror story and is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Data Deletion

All Night of Souls data lives on your device, and you can delete it at any time:

  • Options → "Erase saved game" removes your saved game, or
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored

We hold no copy of your data on any server, so there is nothing further to request.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If a future version adds a third-party service (for example, advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released. The "Last Updated" date at the top indicates when this policy was last revised.
